# CSEE Journal of Power and Energy Systems (CSEE JPES)

## Journal positioning

CSEE JPES is the English-language flagship of the **Chinese Society for Electrical Engineering (CSEE)** (with CEPRI and IEEE), hosted on **IEEE Xplore**. It is a **Q1 power/energy-systems society journal** and an especially strong fit for **Chinese authors** doing broad smart-grid / data-driven / cyber-physical power work: society prestige, IEEE Xplore visibility, and — until recently — very low cost. This is a **fit / framing** tool; official pages win.

- Metrics (as-of 2026-07 — **verify on Clarivate JCR / the journal site**): IF ≈ **5.9** (5-yr 6.4), **Q1 in Engineering, Electrical & Electronic** (Energy & Fuels ≈ Q2 on some cuts); CiteScore ≈ 12.8. Indexed SCIE, Scopus, EI. Quarterly.
- **APC change (important):** historically diamond OA (free); **from 1 Jan 2026 an APC applies — CNY 800 / USD 120 per page** (final PDF page count). Verify at https://www.csee.org.cn/english/APC/.

## When to trigger

- Broad power & energy systems work — renewable integration, power electronics, integrated/multi-energy systems, **big-data / energy cyber-physical / data-driven / smart-grid AI** — needing a Q1 society venue.
- A Chinese power+CS author wants IEEE Xplore visibility and society standing at moderate cost.

## Scope & fit

- All aspects of power & energy systems, **explicitly including "sensing technology and big data application" and "energy cyber-physical systems."**
- **Good power×CS/AI fit:** forecasting, optimization, smart grid, EV, cyber-physical security, data-driven operation/control — broader than PCMP (which is protection/control-only).

## Venue-specific calibration

- **Reviewer lens:** power & energy systems specialists; ~**40% are desk-rejected at preliminary examination**, so scope fit + clarity + significance must be evident early.
- Distinctive fingerprint: power & energy systems · renewable integration · power electronics · integrated/multi-energy · big data · energy cyber-physical systems · smart grid · China grid · IEEE Xplore · Q1.
- Official anchor: journal site + IEEE Xplore.

## Method & evidence bar

- Rigorous validation (simulation and/or real grid/field data); clear advance over existing methods; adequate literature grounding (including recent CSEE/IEEE power work).

### Distilled patterns — power-grid open-data corpus (2026-07)

CSEE JPES is the **Q1 smart-grid / data-driven power** destination when the power-system contribution leads (`../../resources/powergrid-open-data-corpus-distill.md`). Open benchmarks that help desk-reject survival: Grid2Op/L2RPN control, PGLib/OPFData learning-OPF, SimBench DER, NERC-report NLP only if reliability/ops insight is primary. Generic Informer-on-ETT without grid ops narrative → Energy Reports / Energies / IEEE Access.

## Structure & house style

- Original research; review articles; **rapid communications** (fast-track for significant findings). Word/docx templates (regular + rapid comms). Submission via **ScholarOne** (mc03.manuscriptcentral.com/cjpes).

## APC, open access & indexing

- **APC from 2026: CNY 800 / USD 120 per page** (per-page, so long papers cost more — but far cheaper than most flat gold-OA APCs). **Verify current amount/waivers at csee.org.cn/english/APC/.** SCIE + Scopus + EI; Q1.

## Review process & timeline

- **Single-blind**, ≥3 external referees; reviewers asked to respond in 30 days; **first decision typically ~60–80 days (~2–2.7 months)**; submission-to-publication ~26 weeks; usually 1–2 revision rounds. ~40% desk-reject rate.

## Special / topical

- Special issues on power/energy themes under society editorial oversight; same review bar.

## Official-cycle checklist

- Confirm current **per-page APC and any waivers**, scope, rapid-communication eligibility, template, and JCR IF/quartile on the journal site / IEEE Xplore / Clarivate.
- If the live official instructions conflict with this skill, the official instructions win.

## Pre-submission self-check

- [ ] Clear, significant power/energy-systems contribution stated up front (to survive the ~40% desk-reject gate).
- [ ] For data-driven work, the **power-system** contribution (not just the algorithm) is the point.
- [ ] Rigorous validation with comparison to existing methods; recent literature covered.
- [ ] Correct article type (consider **rapid communication** if the finding is significant and time-sensitive).
- [ ] Aware of the per-page APC (budget for page count).

## Common desk-reject triggers

- Weak significance / unclear contribution (hits the ~40% preliminary-rejection gate).
- Pure algorithm paper with thin power-system contribution; inadequate validation.
- Out of power/energy-systems scope.

## Re-routing decision

- Protection/control/fault/stability angle → **PCMP** (free, faster, higher IF).
- Higher-selectivity: **IEEE T-Power Systems, T-Smart Grid, T-Sustainable Energy; Applied Energy**.
- Faster/cheaper broad: **IEEE Access, MDPI Energies; MPCE; OAJPE; iEnergy**.
