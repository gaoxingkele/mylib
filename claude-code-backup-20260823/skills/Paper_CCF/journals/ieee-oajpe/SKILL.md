# IEEE Open Access Journal of Power and Energy (OAJPE)

## Journal positioning

OAJPE is the **IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES)** gold-OA journal (on IEEE Xplore). It is the **IEEE-branded fallback** for power-systems authors who want Xplore indexing and IEEE PES standing and can pay the APC — chosen for brand/visibility rather than for cost or speed (it is neither free nor the fastest here). This is a **fit / framing** tool; the live IEEE Author Portal wins.

- Metrics (as-of 2026-07 — **verify on Clarivate JCR / IEEE**): IF ≈ **2.8** (JCR 2024); **Scimago Q1** in Electrical/Electronic and Energy/Power (JCR quartile borderline Q1/Q2 — verify); CiteScore ≈ 2.9. Indexed SCIE, Scopus, EI.

## When to trigger

- Power-systems work (planning, design, operation, control of generation/T&D/storage/usage; PMU/data analytics; renewable integration) that wants an IEEE-branded OA home and can pay the APC.
- PCMP scope (protection/control) doesn't fit and CSEE JPES isn't preferred.

## Scope & fit

- Planning, design, operation, and control of equipment and power systems for generation, transmission, distribution, storage, and usage of electric energy — academic and practice-oriented.
- **Power×CS/AI fit is implicit, not headlined:** the scope leans equipment/operation, but the journal does publish data-driven work (e.g., GAN-based synthetic PMU data, AI for apparatus). Fit is decent for smart grid / PMU analytics / optimization, weaker signal than a dedicated smart-grid title.

## Venue-specific calibration

- **Reviewer lens:** power engineering specialists; soundness + practical relevance.
- Distinctive fingerprint: power systems · transmission & distribution · smart grid · PMU/data analytics · reliability · renewable integration · operation & control · IEEE PES · gold OA.
- Official anchor: IEEE Xplore OAJPE page + IEEE Author Portal.

## Method & evidence bar

- Sound, reproducible methods; validation on realistic power-system cases/data; clear practical or theoretical advance.

### Distilled patterns — power-grid open-data corpus (2026-07)

Prefer OAJPE when the contribution is **power-system-first** (planning, ops, PMU analytics, renewable integration on realistic cases) rather than a generic DL bake-off on ETT (`../../resources/powergrid-open-data-corpus-distill.md`). Named cases that read well: RTS-GMLC, MATPOWER/PGLib, ACTIVSg/TAMU, MISO MTEP-style planning tables, synthetic PMU. If the story is “new Transformer for multivariate series” with a thin grid sentence → IEEE Access / Energies.

## Structure & house style

- Research articles + extended conference papers; **IEEE two-column template**; submission via the **IEEE Author Portal** (ieee.atyponrex.com/journal/oajpe), not ScholarOne.

## APC, open access & indexing

- **APC ≈ US$2,160** (2026 submissions; rises yearly) — **verify on IEEE**. IEEE member 5% / Society member 20% discounts; low-income-country waivers. Gold OA; SCIE + Scopus + EI.

## Review process & timeline

- Single-blind, ≥2 reviewers, plagiarism screen. **Median to first decision not officially published — verify on the IEEE Author Portal** (aggregator estimate ~10 weeks submission-to-publication, treat as soft). Rejected papers barred from resubmission for 3 months; second rejection final.

## Official-cycle checklist

- Confirm current **APC + discounts**, template, Author Portal submission link, scope, and JCR IF/quartile on IEEE.
- If the live official instructions conflict with this skill, the official instructions win.

## Pre-submission self-check

- [ ] Clear power-systems contribution with realistic-case validation.
- [ ] You want IEEE brand/Xplore and can pay ~US$2,160 (checked discounts).
- [ ] Not a better fit for PCMP (protection/control, free+fast) or CSEE JPES (Q1, cheaper).
- [ ] IEEE two-column template; submitting via IEEE Author Portal.

## Common desk-reject triggers

- Weak/insufficient validation; out of power scope; incremental with thin practical relevance.

## Re-routing decision

- Protection/control → **PCMP** (free, faster, higher IF). Broad smart-grid/data-driven → **CSEE JPES** (Q1, cheaper) or **IEEE Access / MDPI Energies**.
- Higher-selectivity: **IEEE T-Smart Grid, T-Power Systems, T-Sustainable Energy**. Siblings: **IEEE OJ of Power Electronics / Industrial Electronics**.
