# Heliyon (Cell Press / Elsevier)

## Journal positioning

Heliyon (Cell Press/Elsevier, est. 2015, ISSN 2405-8440, gold OA CC BY) is a large multidisciplinary **soundness-based megajournal** with **dedicated named sections** — including **Heliyon Engineering** and **Heliyon Computer Science** — and a **lower APC (~US$1,950)** than Scientific Reports. It is a plausible broad/soundness-based home or re-route for engineering/power/CS work. **Material caution: its Web of Science indexing has been ON HOLD since Sept 2024 and a large retraction surge hit in 2025** — confirm live indexing before relying on it. This is a **fit / framing** tool; official pages + Clarivate status win.

- Metrics (as-of 2026-07 — **verify**): IF ≈ **3.4–3.6** (historically), quartile **uncertain due to the WoS hold**; CiteScore ≈ 4. **Scopus: active. Web of Science/SCIE: ON HOLD since Sept 2024 (unresolved into 2026) — indexing/IF at risk.** APC ≈ **US$1,950** (verify).

## When to trigger / scope & section fit

- Broad, sound, cross-disciplinary work — especially via **Heliyon Engineering** (electrical/electronic, mechanical, civil, energy/power) and **Heliyon Computer Science** (ML, DL, AI, cybersecurity, IoT, NLP, image classification, simulation).
- A **re-route target** for solid papers rejected elsewhere for "lack of novelty"; accepts incremental/null/replication work.

## Venue-specific calibration

- **Reviewer lens:** section editors + external referees; evaluation centers on **scientific/technical validity, not novelty**. Fingerprint: Cell Press megajournal · sectioned (Engineering, Computer Science) · soundness-based · CC BY · lower APC · Scopus-indexed · **WoS on-hold since 2024** · retraction surge · integrity scrutiny.

## Method & evidence bar / house style

- Sound methods and reporting; mandatory ethics/data-availability statements; section-specific guidelines. Elsevier templates; submission via **Editorial Manager** (Cell Press/ScienceDirect Heliyon portal).

### Distilled patterns — power-grid open-data corpus (2026-07)

Technically, many soundness-based power×ML papers could land in Heliyon Engineering/CS — **but given the WoS hold, do not recommend Heliyon for authors who need SCI indexing** (`../../resources/powergrid-open-data-corpus-distill.md`). Prefer Scientific Reports / IEEE Access / Energies / Applied Sciences from the same corpus.

## Review process & timeline

- Section editors + external review (historically single-blind; some sections may offer double-blind — verify). Soundness-based. **Speed moderate and variable by section** (weeks to a few months to first decision); the post-2024 quality crackdown/internal audit may have slowed handling. 1–2 revision rounds.

## Fit signals, reputation nuance & THE coverage warning

- **Submit when:** broad, sound, cross-disciplinary engineering/CS work; want the dedicated Engineering/CS sections and a lower APC; a soundness-based re-route; negative/replication studies.
- **Reputation nuance — material caution (be factual):** Heliyon is legitimately **Scopus-indexed**, but underwent a **research-integrity crisis**: **Clarivate put it "On Hold" in Web of Science (Sept 2024), unresolved into 2026** (during a hold, new articles are not indexed and the journal is under re-evaluation), plus a **retraction surge (~392 papers in 2025 vs 26 in 2024).** Many committees now discount or view it warily. **If SCIE indexing / a stable Impact Factor matters for the author's evaluation (评职/毕业), do NOT assume it is currently WoS-indexed — confirm live status on the Clarivate Master Journal List first.**
- **Common rejections:** methodological weakness, poor reporting, out-of-section scope, integrity/ethics concerns.

## Official-cycle checklist / pre-submission self-check

- Open cell.com/heliyon + sections; **and check the Clarivate Master Journal List for current WoS coverage.** Verify APC, section scope, article types. Official pages win.
- [ ] **Confirmed current WoS/SCIE status** (the decisive check — hold means new papers may not get an IF). [ ] Sound methods/reporting; correct section (Engineering / Computer Science). [ ] Ethics/data statements complete. [ ] Author aware of the indexing risk vs. cheaper APC trade-off.

## Re-routing decision

- Stably-indexed same-model alternatives: **Scientific Reports, PLOS ONE, MDPI Applied Sciences / Electronics / Energies, IEEE Access** (faster for engineering/power/CS). Higher tier: **Nature Communications, PNAS Nexus**; power/electrical: **IET, IEEE Transactions**.
