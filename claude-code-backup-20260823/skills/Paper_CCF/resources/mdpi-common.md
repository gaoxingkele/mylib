# MDPI journals — common model (shared reference)

Applies to all MDPI titles profiled in this skill (Energies, Electronics, Applied Sciences, Information, Algorithms, Future Internet, Remote Sensing, Atmosphere, Symmetry, Sensors, Machines, Mathematics, Sustainability, …) and most other MDPI journals. Journal-specific facts (scope, sections, APC amount, IF/quartile) live in each `journals/<slug>/SKILL.md`. All numbers are **as-of 2026-07 and change yearly — verify on the live journal page** (`https://www.mdpi.com/journal/<name>`); the official page always wins.

## The model in one paragraph
MDPI journals are **fully gold open-access, author-pays (APC after acceptance), single-blind, fast-turnaround, high-volume** journals. Manuscripts are submitted through **SuSy** (susy.mdpi.com), pre-checked by an in-house Managing/Assistant Editor (scope, format, English, iThenticate plagiarism screen), then handled by an Academic Editor (Editor-in-Chief, a Section Editor, or a Special-Issue Guest Editor) with ≥2 external reviewers. Median time to first decision is typically **~15–17 days**; acceptance-to-publication **~3 days**. Revision windows are short (often ~10 days minor, ~10–21 major), usually 1–2 rounds.

## Two orthogonal topical structures
- **Sections** — permanent topical divisions, each with its own Section Editor-in-Chief and board. Your submission routes to a Section; matching the right Section matters for handling and fit.
- **Special Issues (SIs)** — time-bound thematic collections led by **Guest Editors**. A very large share of MDPI output publishes via SIs; thousands run concurrently. SI papers get the same single-blind review, same APC, and identical indexing as regular submissions.

## Author implications & risks (advise on these)
- **Guest-Editor / SI invitation emails are common and high-volume.** They are legitimate but vary in rigor by Guest Editor — vet the GE's standing and the SI's genuine scope fit before accepting.
- **Scope fit is the #1 desk-reject lever.** A mismatched Section/SI is rejected or rerouted fast (MDPI desk-rejects out-of-scope work in ~7 days).
- Short review windows reward manuscripts that are **already complete and polished** at submission.
- **Reputation nuance — present it factually and fairly:** these titles are **genuinely SCIE- and Scopus-indexed** with real quartile standing, not on Beall's-successor or DOAJ blocklists. MDPI is nonetheless debated in the "predatory-adjacent / quality-control" conversation because of very rapid growth, heavy Special-Issue reliance, aggressive Guest-Editor solicitation, short review timelines, and self-citation scrutiny (Clarivate has periodically re-evaluated MDPI titles). Reception is **field- and committee-dependent** — some hiring/tenure/评职 committees discount MDPI venues. Bottom line: legitimate, indexed, "counts" in most systems, but choose deliberately (scope fit + SI vetting) and weigh the target audience.

## House style (all MDPI)
- Templates: **Word and LaTeX** (MDPI class; Overleaf gallery). Single-column submission.
- Structure: IMRaD (Introduction, Materials & Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions).
- References: **MDPI numbered style** (ACS-like, bracketed `[1]`, sequential).
- Mandatory: **Data Availability Statement**, author contributions (CRediT), funding, conflicts of interest, IRB/ethics + informed consent where relevant.
- Preprints allowed/encouraged (Preprints.org); not counted as prior publication.
- COPE member; ICMJE/COPE guidance; iThenticate similarity screening; undisclosed-AI-use and image-manipulation policies apply.

## APC discounts/waivers (all MDPI)
IOAP (Institutional Open Access Program) discounts; affiliated-society member discounts; reviewer/editor vouchers; invited papers often waived; case-by-case waivers at editorial discretion (request before/at submission). No submission fee; APC charged only after acceptance.
