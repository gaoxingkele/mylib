# Design a Confound-Isolating Diagnostic
_id: `controlled_diagnostic_design` · journal=`ieee-internet-of-things-journal` · primary_count=3_

**Operational signature**. confound → controlled instances → isolate true property

**When to apply in `ieee-internet-of-things-journal`**. When accepted local papers execute this move.

## Success conditions
- Named bottleneck closed by the operational signature.
- Venue-appropriate evidence (baselines/case/figures).
- Explicit differentiation vs nearest prior.

## Failure modes
- Mechanical wrapper of known modules.
- Confounded or single-toy evidence.
- Untouched true bottleneck.
