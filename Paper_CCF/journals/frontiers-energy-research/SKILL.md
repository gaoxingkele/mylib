# Frontiers in Energy Research

## Journal positioning

Frontiers in Energy Research (Frontiers Media, est. 2013, ISSN 2296-598X, gold OA CC BY) is a broad energy journal organized into ~16 **specialty sections**, notable for its **distinctive collaborative, transparent, interactive review** (named reviewers in a real-time Review Forum). Mid-tier IF; smart when you want a fast, dialogue-based review and section-aligned energy fit. This is a **fit / framing** tool; the official Frontiers page wins.

- Metrics (as-of 2026-07 — **verify at https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/energy-research**): IF ≈ **2.58** (2024; note Frontiers-wide IF cuts in JCR 2023), **Q2** Energy & Fuels (some sources Q3 — verify); CiteScore ≈ 5 (official page may show a higher live tracker ~6.3). SCIE + Scopus + DOAJ. APC ≈ **CHF 2,695** (Type A) / CHF 2,195 (Type B).

## When to trigger / scope & section fit

- Section-aligned energy work; **power×CS/AI fit routes mainly through the Smart Grids, Process and Energy Systems Engineering, and Sustainable Energy Systems sections** (grid optimization, demand response, forecasting, energy-systems modeling). The journal is more chemistry/materials/renewables-weighted on the pure power axis, so **targeting the right section matters.**

## Venue-specific calibration

- **Reviewer lens:** section experts using a standardized quality-assessment questionnaire, then interactive dialogue. Fingerprint: gold-OA CC BY · collaborative/interactive review · named reviewers · Review Forum dialogue · AIRA integrity AI · ~90-day review · section-based · mid-tier IF · Q2 Energy & Fuels.

## Method & evidence bar / house style

- Sound, section-appropriate methodology; passes the AIRA integrity/scope pre-check. Frontiers Word template; submission and review run inside the proprietary Frontiers platform.

## Distilled patterns — power-grid open-data corpus (2026-07)

Corpus routes **BESS balancing / aFRR / European market inputs and DR-style grid optimization** toward Frontiers when authors want interactive review (`../../resources/powergrid-open-data-corpus-distill.md`).

- Prefer sections: **Smart Grids**, Process & Energy Systems Engineering, Sustainable Energy Systems.
- Evidence that fits the Review Forum: named open inputs (M5BAT companions, Finland aFRR+weather, European balancing BESS packs) + techno-economic or reserve-performance metrics + limitations you can defend in dialogue.
- Weaker fit: pure ETT bake-offs or generic OPF solvers with no energy-systems section narrative — use Energies / IEEE Access instead.

## Review process (distinctive) & timeline

1. **AIRA** AI integrity/plagiarism/scope pre-check. 2. **Independent Review** — reviewers assess independently via a standardized questionnaire. 3. **Interactive Review** — the defining feature: authors and named reviewers converge in a real-time **Review Forum** dialogue. 4. **Editor endorsement** once reviewers explicitly approve. 5. **Named transparency** — reviewer/editor names published on the article.
- **Speed:** Frontiers states **average review < ~90 days**; typical **submission-to-publication ≈ 12 weeks**.

## Fit signals, reputation nuance & pitfalls

- **Submit when:** section-aligned energy paper; want a fast, transparent, dialogue-based review; value guaranteed OA visibility.
- **Reputation nuance (be factual, it is contested):** Frontiers was **added to Beall's "questionable" list in 2015** (controversial; the list was later taken offline). Critics argue the interactive model makes outright rejection harder and question quality consistency. **Counterweight:** legitimately indexed in **SCIE, Scopus, DOAJ**, with named reviewers on record — mainstream OA outlet. Present as **reputable-but-debated**, not a consensus "predatory" finding; weigh committee perceptions.
- **Common rejections:** out-of-section scope, AIRA integrity flags, insufficient novelty/methodology per the reviewer checklist.

## Official-cycle checklist / pre-submission self-check

- Open the journal + publishing-fees + review-system pages; verify APC (by article type), IF/quartile, the section list, and pick the correct section. Official pages win.
- [ ] Manuscript maps to a specific **section** (ideally Smart Grids / Energy Systems Engineering for power+CS). [ ] Passes integrity/scope expectations. [ ] Prepared to engage the interactive Review Forum. [ ] APC budgeted by article type.

## Re-routing decision

- Faster/section-based siblings: **MDPI Energies**. Broad fast OA: **IEEE Access, Energy Reports**. Higher bar (hybrid): **Applied Energy, Energy, Renewable Energy**. Power-specific: **CSEE JPES, PCMP, OAJPE**.
