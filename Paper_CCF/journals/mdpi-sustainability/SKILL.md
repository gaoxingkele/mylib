# Sustainability (MDPI)

## Journal positioning

Sustainability (est. 2009, ISSN 2071-1050, semimonthly, gold OA) is a very broad, high-volume cross-disciplinary journal on environmental, economic, and social **sustainability and sustainable development**. Gating fit rule: **the sustainability / SDG / environmental / energy-transition / socioeconomic-impact angle must be substantive**, not a token "sustainable" mention on an otherwise pure engineering/ML paper. Read `../../resources/mdpi-common.md` first for the shared MDPI model; the reputation nuance (breadth/volume) is most acute here, so sharpen the impact narrative.

- Metrics (as-of 2026-07 — **verify at https://www.mdpi.com/journal/sustainability**): IF ≈ **3.3** (Q2, Environmental Studies / Green & Sustainable Sci & Tech; sources split ~3.3–4.1), CiteScore ≈ **8**; APC ≈ **CHF 2,400**; median ≈ **16.9 days** to first decision. Indexed SCIE, Scopus.

## When to trigger / scope & section fit

- Technical or policy work with an explicit sustainability framing: renewable integration, smart grid, energy policy, demand response, EV/transport, sustainability-driven optimization, environmental/socioeconomic impact.
- Sections include: **Energy Sustainability**; Environmental Sustainability & Applications; Economic & Business Aspects; **Sustainable Transportation**; Green Building; Sustainable Agriculture; Urban/Rural Development; Resources & Sustainable Utilization; Waste/Recycling; Water Management. **Verify current list at `/journal/sustainability/sections`.**
- **Power×CS/energy fit:** grid/renewables/EV/energy-policy work with a genuine sustainability contribution.

## Venue-specific calibration

- **Reviewer lens:** "Is the sustainability contribution substantive and evidenced?" Fingerprint: sustainability · SDGs · renewable energy · smart grid · energy policy · environmental impact · circular economy · sustainable transport.

## Method & evidence bar / house style

- Sound methodology; the sustainability/impact claim must be supported (quantified environmental/economic/social outcome), not asserted. MDPI Word/LaTeX template, IMRaD, MDPI numbered refs (see `../../resources/mdpi-common.md`).

## Distilled patterns — power-grid open-data corpus (2026-07)

OA map tags Sustainability for **renewable integration, DR/SGSC trials, transmission expansion, and climate–energy scenarios** when an SDG/impact angle is real (9 dataset rows; `../../resources/powergrid-open-data-corpus-distill.md`).

- Acceptable impact evidence: CO₂/curtailment reduced, renewable share uplift, resilience hours saved, socioeconomic DR participation — quantified, not slogan.
- Desk-risk: MAE-only load forecasting or OPF papers that sprinkle “sustainable grid” in the title. Re-route those to Energies / IEEE Access.
- Best Sections for this corpus: Energy Sustainability; Sustainable Transportation (EV/ACN); Resources & Sustainable Utilization (planning).

## APC / review / Special Issues

- APC ≈ CHF 2,400 after acceptance (verify). Single-blind, ≥2 reviewers, ~16.9 d first decision, 1–2 short revision rounds. Very heavy Special-Issue model — pick an on-scope SI; vet Guest-Editor invitations.

## Official-cycle checklist / pre-submission self-check

- Open `/journal/sustainability`, `/instructions`, `/apc`, `/sections`, `/special_issues`, `/stats`; verify APC, IF/quartile, Section list. Official pages win.
- [ ] **Substantive sustainability/SDG/impact contribution** (not decorative). [ ] The impact claim is quantified/evidenced. [ ] Correct Section / on-scope SI. [ ] Impact narrative sharpened for a broad, mixed audience.

## Common desk-reject triggers

- Pure engineering/optimization/ML with only a token "sustainable" mention; sustainability claim asserted but not evidenced; out of Section scope.

## Re-routing decision

- **MDPI Energies** (energy-technical focus), **Renewable & Sustainable Energy Reviews (Elsevier)**, **Journal of Cleaner Production (Elsevier)**, **Environments (MDPI)**, **IEEE Access**.
