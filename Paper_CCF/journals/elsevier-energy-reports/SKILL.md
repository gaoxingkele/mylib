# Energy Reports (Elsevier)

## Journal positioning

Energy Reports (est. 2015, ISSN 2352-4847, gold OA) is Elsevier's broad, high-volume energy journal — the gold-OA **companion to Energy and Applied Energy** (it receives editor-transferred manuscripts from those titles). Strong home for **applied energy / power-systems / AI-for-energy** work where the **energy application (not the algorithm) is the contribution**, with relatively fast OA turnaround. This is a **fit / framing** tool; the official ScienceDirect page wins.

- Metrics (as-of 2026-07 — **verify at https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/energy-reports**): IF ≈ **6.3–6.6** (2024; historically volatile — 4.9 in 2021 then wobble), **Q1 Energy & Fuels**; SCIE + Scopus + EI + DOAJ (verify current WoS status). APC ≈ **US$3,040** (Elsevier list; some sources ~$2,360 — **get the personalized quote at submission**).

## When to trigger / scope & fit

- Applied energy across renewables, storage, hydrogen/fuel cells, generation, conversion, buildings/efficiency, energy economics/policy — **and power systems, smart grids, energy informatics, forecasting, and AI/ML/optimization applied to energy.**
- **Power×CS/AI fit is strong** for load/renewable forecasting, grid optimization, demand-side management, ML-for-energy — provided the energy contribution is the point.
- Also the natural in-house landing spot for manuscripts **transferred/rejected from Energy or Applied Energy**.

## Venue-specific calibration

- **Reviewer lens:** energy-domain specialists; the energy contribution + validation matter more than algorithmic novelty. Fingerprint: gold-OA · fast review · Elsevier companion journal · broad energy · power systems & smart grids · AI/forecasting-for-energy · Q1 Energy & Fuels · special-issue heavy.

## Method & evidence bar / house style

- Solid energy-domain validation (real data / realistic cases); adequate benchmarking. Standard Elsevier structure; LaTeX/Word; submission via **Editorial Manager**.

## Distilled patterns — power-grid open-data corpus (2026-07)

Curated OA map ranks Energy Reports among top targets for **load / price / renewable forecasting and applied AI-for-energy** (11 dataset rows; see `../../resources/powergrid-open-data-corpus-distill.md`).

- Lead with the **energy decision** (dispatch, trading, DER sizing, adequacy) that the model enables — not Transformer architecture trivia.
- Public benchmarks that travel well here: OPSD, ENTSO-E Transparency, Elia/Ausgrid/UCI loads, NSRDB/renewables.ninja CF, ERA5-driven supply–demand.
- Evidence floor observed in sibling applied-energy literature: ≥3 baselines for forecasting, MAE/RMSE/MAPE, and either a real market/operator case or a clearly motivated synthetic scenario; sensitivity on horizon/features helps.
- Natural cascade lander after Energy / Applied Energy transfer — keep the energy contribution paragraph intact during rewrite.

## APC, open access & review process

- APC ≈ US$3,040 (verify; Research4Life waivers; institutional agreements). Single-blind; **markets rapid review**; typical **submission-to-publication ≈ 16 weeks** (some ~19), with a tight **~20-day revised-manuscript window**. First-decision time not officially published — verify (community reports weeks to ~1–2 months).

## Fit signals, reputation nuance & pitfalls

- **Submit when:** solid applied energy / power-systems / AI-for-energy work; want fast OA; or your paper was transferred from Energy/Applied Energy.
- **Reputation nuance (be factual):** legitimate Elsevier gold-OA journal, **Q1, SCIE+Scopus indexed**; but it saw **2024 retractions tied to fictitious author identities/manipulated affiliations**, and high-volume Elsevier OA titles face general Clarivate scrutiny (peer title Heliyon was put on hold). No delisting of Energy Reports is on record — **verify current WoS coverage.** Reputable, but watch the integrity narrative.
- **Common rejections:** thin energy-domain contribution (pure algorithm), scope drift, weak validation, poor English/structure.

## Official-cycle checklist / pre-submission self-check

- Open the ScienceDirect journal + OA-options pages; verify APC, IF/quartile, current WoS status, article types. Official pages win.
- [ ] The **energy contribution** (not the algorithm) is the point, with domain validation. [ ] Fits a fast-OA plan; APC budgeted (verify amount). [ ] English/structure clean; ready for a ~20-day revision window.

## Re-routing decision

- Up (higher bar, hybrid): **Applied Energy, Energy, Renewable Energy**. Siblings: **IEEE Access, MDPI Energies, Energy Science & Engineering (Wiley OA), IET Energy Systems Integration**. Power-specific: **CSEE JPES, PCMP, OAJPE**.
