# Rejection re-audit protocol — 2026-10-31

Use with standards-and-evidence.md and ../../../resources/comparative-multiscale-review.md (paths relative to this references directory).

## Evidence identity precedes diagnosis

Bind decision letter, portal attachment inventory, uploaded ZIP/PDF, current source and historical baseline separately. A local submission ZIP without supplements does not establish that the portal lacked separately uploaded supplements. A current draft cannot retroactively explain a rejection of another version. Search absence is scoped absence, not fabrication evidence.

Label findings as directly verified defect, inherited finding awaiting replication, scientific risk, or documented editorial reason. Do not rank inferred risks as known rejection causes.

## Review the previous review

Reopen the exact cited paragraph before preserving a critical finding. Check whether allegedly missing mechanism explanations already exist. Separate no explanation from plausible but untested explanation and from quantitative causal attribution.

Paired accuracy differences require discordant pairs and appropriate dependence handling; a single-proportion standard error cannot establish a paired difference is noise. Schema/report-series units and repeated queries must remain distinct. Same coverage with different answered sets estimates policy risk, not accuracy on identical cases. Budget ceilings do not imply equal realized cost.

Deterministic agents are not automatically invalid agents. Judge information access, state, authority, communication and incremental value rather than requiring one LLM call per role. Expert annotation alone does not establish human-subjects research status; examine actual design and institutional determination. Do not invent an ethics prohibition.

## Latest sample and comparison

Archive query, cutoff, date field, tie breaker, DOI, publisher date, article type, file hash and reading coverage. Crossref latest indexed records are not guaranteed publisher-wide latest publications. Same-day ties may be resolved deterministically but disclosed. Retain reviews/conceptual papers in chronological samples, then stratify applicability rather than pretending all ten are algorithm benchmarks.

Record download, extraction, paragraph reading, visual review, citation checking and reproduction separately. Abstract reading cannot support full-text coverage claims.

Published samples are useful counterexamples to claimed page/figure/citation quotas, not a replacement acceptance rule. Never require target-journal citations for their own sake. Dense numbers and negative results are cues for readability and evidence review, not automatic defects.

## Stronger scientific comparison

For each central claim identify an alternative explanation and the smallest discriminating control: same-input simple rule, randomized/removed/contradictory evidence, matched output length and score scale, matched realized inference budget, independent semantic labels, or a positive control establishing detection capability. Map task → construct → intervention → endpoint → inference → limit.

Distinguish mechanism inactivity, implementation failure, weak construct measurement and evidence of bounded lack of benefit. Overlapping datasets and budgets are not independent replications. Acknowledging limitations improves honesty, not external validity. A negative-result study needs useful diagnostic knowledge beyond a long history of failed revisions.
