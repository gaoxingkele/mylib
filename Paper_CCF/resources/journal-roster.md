## Conference-vs-journal quick guide
- **Fast OA, sound-but-not-groundbreaking** → IEEE Access / MDPI / CMC / Discover Computing.
- **Selective high IF IoT / storage** → IEEE IoT-J / Journal of Energy Storage.
- **Novelty + community** → CS conference module or selective IEEE Transactions.
- **APC matters:** confirm current APC and institutional discounts before advising.
